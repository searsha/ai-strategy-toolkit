## Implementation Considerations

Before moving forward, consider:

* Who owns the initiative?
* Who is accountable for AI-generated outputs?
* What data will the system access?
* What controls are required?
* Where must human review remain?
* How will employees or stakeholders be prepared for the change?
* How will success be measured?
* What conditions would trigger reevaluation or discontinuation?


## Success Measures
