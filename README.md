# AI Strategy Toolkit

A practical collection of frameworks designed to help organizations evaluate, prioritize, govern, and implement AI initiatives responsibly.

This repository focuses on the strategic side of AI adoption — helping leaders move from experimentation to intentional, scalable implementation.

## Purpose

Organizations are increasingly exploring AI, but the challenge is rarely just identifying what AI *can* do. The more important questions are:

* Which opportunities are worth pursuing?
* Where will AI create meaningful business value?
* Is the organization ready to implement the solution?
* What risks and governance requirements need to be addressed?
* Where should humans remain involved?
* How should impact be measured?

This toolkit is designed to provide structured approaches for answering those questions.

## Frameworks

### AI Use Case Prioritization

A structured approach for evaluating and comparing potential AI initiatives based on business value, feasibility, organizational readiness, risk, and measurable impact.

### AI Readiness Assessment

A framework for evaluating whether an organization has the strategy, data, technology, governance, processes, and workforce capabilities needed to adopt AI effectively.

### Human-in-the-Loop Framework

A decision framework for determining where human review, judgment, oversight, or approval should remain within AI-enabled workflows.

## Templates

Practical templates for evaluating AI opportunities and applying the frameworks to real-world use cases.

## Examples

Sample evaluations demonstrating how the frameworks can be applied to common organizational AI initiatives.
